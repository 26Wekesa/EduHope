ABOUT BOLD FOUNDATION

Every boy deserves the support to lead with confidence.

B.O.L.D. means Boys Of Leadership & Dignity. Bold Foundation works to empower, support, and advocate for the holistic development of the boy child.

Founded in September 2025 by Sackland K. Makanga, the foundation's CEO.

Explore our programmes

Why we exist

We believe every boy should have opportunities to build confidence, leadership, emotional intelligence, and a sense of responsibility.

The foundation works with schools and communities to support boys' development.

Our mission centers the boy child. Some activities bring boys and girls together so they can understand one another and learn to thrive together.

Our vision

A world where every boy grows into a confident, respectful, and purpose-driven man who contributes positively to society.

Our values

Kindness, respect, integrity, accountability, honesty, and collaboration guide our work.

LEADERSHIP

Sackland K. Makanga, Founder and CEO

Born in 2001 in Ikolomani, Kakamega County, Makanga is the founder and CEO of Bold Foundation. He is studying for a Bachelor of Education in English and Literature at Kibabii University.

Makanga is also the author of the published book Beyond the Timetable.

He is also a poet; his collection, The Fading Bond, is unpublished.
