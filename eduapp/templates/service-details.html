Mentorship Programme

Helping young people grow into confident, responsible, and purpose-driven leaders.

Through mentorship, leadership sessions, public speaking, and community engagement, Bold Foundation equips both boys and girls with the confidence, emotional intelligence, and values they need to lead with dignity while responding to today’s environmental and socio-economic challenges.

Programme Overview

A mentorship experience built on leadership, dignity, inclusion, and social responsibility.

The mentorship programme is designed to support young people in schools and communities as they grow through adolescence into responsible, aware, and resilient leaders. We create safe, encouraging spaces where they can learn, reflect, and build positive habits for life.

Each participant is guided through sessions focused on self-awareness, discipline, public speaking, leadership, emotional intelligence, gender equity, environmental care, and emerging social and economic realities. We believe mentorship should not only inform, but transform.

Programme Goals

  • Build confidence and resilience
  • Strengthen leadership and participation
  • Promote dignity, equality, and respect
  • Address emerging social and economic challenges
  • Encourage environmental stewardship and community responsibility
What We Offer

Mentorship that speaks to the realities of young people today.

Peer and Adult Mentorship

Young people are matched with mentors who guide them through life challenges, purpose discovery, and positive behavior change across school, home, and community settings.

Public Speaking & Confidence

We help both boys and girls build voice, clarity, and confidence through speaking sessions, presentations, and leadership practice.

Community Outreach

We conduct outreach programmes in schools and communities on climate action, youth employability, social issues, and community well-being.

Who is this programme for?

Young people in school

We support learners who need guidance, confidence, mentorship, and positive role models, including both boys and girls.

Community youth groups

We partner with communities to create mentorship circles and leadership-building sessions on social, economic, and environmental issues.

Young leaders preparing for the future

We help emerging leaders grow in discipline, responsibility, communication, environmental awareness, and service to others.

How the programme works

  1. We identify boys and youth in need of support and guidance.
  2. We match them with mentors and facilitators who model leadership and integrity.
  3. We run structured sessions around confidence, values, communication, and purpose.
  4. We track progress and celebrate growth through community engagement.

Become part of the mentorship journey.

Whether you want to mentor, support, or partner with Bold Foundation, your contribution helps young people—boys and girls—grow into stronger leaders, environmental stewards, and responsible citizens.
