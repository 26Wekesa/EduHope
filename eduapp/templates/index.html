Boys Of Leadership & Dignity

Empowering Boys. Building Leaders. Shaping Dignity.

Bold Foundation is a community-based organization founded on June 25th, 2025 by Kibabii University public speaking club members. We empower, support, and advocate for the holistic development of the boy child through leadership, dignity, emotional intelligence, and social responsibility.

About Us

A movement shaping confident, respectful men of purpose.

Bold Foundation was born from the lived experience of mentoring communities and schools through public speaking and mentorship programs. Our mission is to nurture leadership, dignity, emotional intelligence, and social responsibility in every boy we reach.

Our vision is a world where every boy grows into a confident, respectful, and purpose-driven man who leads and contributes positively to society.

Mission

To empower, support, and advocate for the holistic development of the boy child.

Vision

To build confident, respectful, and purpose-driven young men.

Our Core Values
Kindness Respect Integrity Accountability Honesty Collaboration
How We Help

Programs rooted in mentorship, leadership, and dignity

Mentorship

We guide boys through mentorship that builds confidence, discipline, leadership, and self-worth.

Public Speaking

We create spaces for boys to speak boldly, express ideas, and grow into articulate leaders.

Community Engagement

We partner with schools and communities to create opportunities for boys to thrive and contribute.
