Every boy deserves the support to lead with confidence.

Founded by members of Kibabii University’s public speaking club, Bold Foundation works with schools and communities to support the holistic development of the boy child.

We envision a world where every boy grows into a confident, respectful, and purpose-driven man who contributes positively to society.

Our mission

To empower, support, and advocate for the holistic development of the boy child.

Our values

Kindness, respect, integrity, accountability, honesty, and collaboration guide our work.

Practical support for boys to speak, lead, and thrive.

Mentorship

Public speaking

Opportunities to express ideas, speak boldly, and grow into articulate leaders.

Community engagement

Partnerships with schools and communities that help boys thrive and contribute.
