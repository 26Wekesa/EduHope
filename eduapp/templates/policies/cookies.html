Status: Draft for legal and technical review. Cookie configuration and all deployed pages should be rechecked before adoption.

1. What we use now

The website uses essential browser storage for security and site functions:

  • csrftoken helps protect form submissions against cross-site request forgery. Django’s current default lifetime is one year; confirm the production setting.
  • sessionid supports server-side sessions, including the temporary Terms acceptance state and payment-flow navigation. Django’s current default lifetime is 14 days; confirm the production setting.
  • bold-cookie-preferences-v1 is stored in this browser’s local storage to remember the visitor’s cookie choice. It is not a cookie and remains until cleared, changed or invalidated by a later preference version.

Essential security and session storage cannot be switched off through the preference controls without affecting site functions.

2. Optional cookies and trackers

No analytics or marketing trackers were found in the site templates reviewed for this draft. The banner’s “Accept all” and “Reject non-essential” choices are recorded, but neither choice currently turns on an optional tracking service. If analytics or marketing tools are added, they must remain blocked until the visitor gives the required consent, and this policy and preference controls must be updated before release.

3. Choosing and changing preferences

The first-visit banner offers “Accept all cookies”, “Reject non-essential”, and “Manage preferences”. Visitors can reopen the controls using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Preferences are saved in browser local storage. If browser settings prevent saving, optional trackers remain disabled.

Clearing site data in the browser removes the saved choice. Essential cookies may still be set when a visitor uses forms or account features.

4. Third-party services

Using the Gemini chatbot sends the submitted question and recent turns to Google’s API. Payment requests send the phone number and transaction details to Safaricom’s M-Pesa service. These are service integrations, not analytics cookies. Read the Privacy Policy for the broader data-processing description.
