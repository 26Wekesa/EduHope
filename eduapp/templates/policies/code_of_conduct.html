Status: Draft for leadership approval. The foundation must assign an owner, train covered people, define investigation steps and confirm proportionate consequences before adoption.

1. Who must follow this Code

This draft covers board and leadership members, staff, mentors, volunteers, contractors, partners and anyone acting in the foundation’s name, at activities and online.

2. Our conduct commitments

  • Act lawfully, honestly, respectfully and in the best interests of children and communities.
  • Respect equality, human dignity, privacy, cultural identity and the right of children to be heard.
  • Use foundation resources and authority only for approved purposes; disclose conflicts of interest.
  • Protect personal, safeguarding and financial information; share it only with authorised people who need it.
  • Use clear, professional boundaries in person, by phone and online. Keep communication transparent and programme-related.
  • Obtain required permission before collecting or publishing a child’s photo, voice, name, story or school information.
  • Raise suspected abuse, exploitation, fraud, discrimination, retaliation or other serious misconduct promptly through a safe channel.

3. Prohibited conduct

  • Abuse, exploitation, harassment, sexual conduct with a child, grooming, bullying, threats, degrading treatment or discrimination.
  • Private or secret contact with a child that bypasses approved programme and guardian safeguards.
  • Requesting or sharing intimate images, using a child’s personal data for personal reasons, or posting identifiable stories without specific authorisation.
  • Offering or requesting money, gifts, favours or private benefits in exchange for access, selection, grades, services or silence.
  • Retaliating against a person who raises a concern in good faith, or concealing, altering or destroying relevant records.
  • Using foundation funds, donation accounts or payment information for personal purposes.

4. Reporting and response

Anyone who receives a child-safety disclosure must listen without investigating, record only necessary facts and escalate promptly. Immediate danger or a legal reporting obligation requires contact with the appropriate authority. Other misconduct should be raised with an authorised lead who is not implicated in the concern.

The foundation has not yet confirmed a dedicated safeguarding officer, independent complaints contact or formal investigation procedure. Until these are approved and communicated, this draft must not be treated as proof that confidential or independent reporting is available. See the Child Safeguarding Policy and Complaints Policy.

5. Acknowledgement and consequences

Before a person begins a covered role, the foundation should provide this Code in an accessible form, explain how to seek guidance and record acknowledgement. Breaches should be assessed fairly, with safety first, appropriate support for affected people, due process and referral to authorities where required. Specific disciplinary steps and appeal arrangements remain to be approved.
