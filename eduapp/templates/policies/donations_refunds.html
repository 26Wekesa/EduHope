Status: Draft. Refund authority, approval controls, receipt process and finance contact require confirmation before this policy is adopted. The foundation has set a target for an initial response to requests within seven calendar days.

1. Current donation route

The public donation flow currently accepts one-time contributions linked to a published event and initiates payment through an M-Pesa STK Push. A payment remains pending until the system receives and verifies a matching successful payment callback. A pending or failed request must not be described as a completed donation.

The foundation’s stated supporter reach is international, while its beneficiary programmes focus on children in Kenya. The current public payment form offers M-Pesa STK Push only; it does not confirm support for every overseas payment method or any donor tax treatment outside Kenya.

Mentor applications are free and do not require a payment. General bank details, recurring donation instructions, tax deductibility and donation receipts have not been confirmed and are not promised by this draft.

2. Safe payment practices

  • Start a donation only from an official Bold Foundation event page and check the amount and event before confirming.
  • Safaricom sends the payment prompt to the phone number entered. The donor enters their PIN directly in the M-Pesa prompt; the foundation will never ask for the PIN or one-time password.
  • Do not send money to an individual account based on an unsolicited message. Report suspected impersonation through a trusted contact route.
  • Safaricom processes the payment and receives the phone and transaction information needed for that service. See the Privacy Policy.

3. Refund requests

If a payment appears duplicated, unauthorised, misdirected or inconsistent with the amount shown, contact boldfoundation59@gmail.com or +254 793 299 818 with the event, approximate time, amount and M-Pesa receipt or transaction reference. Never include an M-Pesa PIN.

The foundation must verify the payment and approve a written refund procedure, including the decision authority, permitted grounds, return method, handling of payment-provider reversals and response target. Those details are not yet confirmed, so this draft does not promise that every donation is refundable or set a final refund deadline. The foundation aims to send an initial response within seven calendar days; a verified refund decision may take longer. Applicable consumer and payment laws remain relevant.

4. Stewardship and records

Donations should be recorded against verified payment status, reconciled against provider records, used for authorised charitable purposes and reported transparently. Access to donor contact details and payment records should be limited. The foundation should publish its approved financial controls, receipt practice, reporting cycle and any restricted-gift rules before soliciting broad public donations.

See the Data Retention Policy draft for unresolved record-retention periods.

5. Concerns

Payment concerns may be sent to the general email above or raised through Complaints and Grievance. A designated finance contact and escalation route still need approval.
