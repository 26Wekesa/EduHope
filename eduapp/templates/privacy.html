Privacy Policy

A clear guide to the information Bold Foundation collects, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you submit when you apply for learner support, book mentorship, apply as a mentor, request a partnership, contact us, or support an event.

Personal Information

  • Names and contact details
  • School, learner, or organisation information
  • Programme preferences and messages
  • Details needed to coordinate support safely

Usage Data

Our website may receive basic browser, device, and page-visit information to help us keep the service reliable and secure.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to requests, coordinate programmes, communicate follow-ups, improve the website, and meet legal, accounting, safeguarding, and fraud-prevention responsibilities.

Primary Uses

  1. Review and respond to applications and bookings
  2. Coordinate mentors, learners, partners, and events
  3. Protect the safety of programme participants
  4. Improve website security and accessibility

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with trusted providers who help operate the website, where required by law, or where necessary to protect a learner, participant, or staff member.

We Never Sell Your Data

Your information is used only for legitimate programme, service, communication, security, or legal purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Encryption

Access to information is limited to people who need it for an approved programme or operational purpose.

Secure Storage

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for programme records, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, subject to legal and safeguarding requirements.

Right to Access

Ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is being used.

Right to Rectification

Request corrections where the information we hold is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion where there is no continuing legal or safeguarding reason to retain it.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use essential session storage to support navigation, security, and functions such as remembering terms acceptance. You can manage cookies through your browser.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Navigation, security, and session functions Session
Analytics Understanding website use As needed
Marketing Not used None

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations, or safeguarding practices change. The latest version will be posted here with its revision date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Bold Foundation and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.
