Rights and legal framework

These drafts are guided by the Constitution of Kenya, including equality, dignity, privacy and children’s rights; Kenya’s data-protection and children’s legislation; the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC); and the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child. The references are starting points for review, not a legal opinion on applicability.

Provisions for counsel to consider include Constitution Articles 2(6), 27, 28, 31 and 53; UNCRC Articles 2, 3, 12, 16, 19, 28 and 34; and African Charter Articles 3, 4, 10, 11, 16, 21 and 27.

  • Constitution of Kenya, 2010
  • Data Protection Act, No. 24 of 2019 and applicable regulations
  • Children Act, No. 29 of 2022
  • UN Convention on the Rights of the Child
  • African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child
  • Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)
