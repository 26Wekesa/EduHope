OUR IMPACT

Activities, partnerships, and community action.

The highlights below are activities reported by the foundation. This page does not yet include measured outcome statistics or annual reports.

Mental wellness outreach

Bold Foundation participated with partner organizations in a mental wellness activity at Masinde Muliro University. Participants spent time with orphans, shared a meal, and donated basic hygiene items including tissue, soap, and sanitary towels.

Tree planting

Tree planting is part of the foundation's climate and community action activities.

Mentorship partnership

On 11 October 2026, Bold Foundation partnered with Kibabii University Mentorship Club for a mentorship event held in connection with the Day of the Girl Child.

Community clean-ups

Market clean-ups are among the community outreach activities connected to climate action.
